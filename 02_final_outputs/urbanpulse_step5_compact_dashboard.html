Are there structural or provenance anomalies?

Key structural finding: all 1,600 canonical timestamps are unique after deduplication, with one row per sampled timestamp. This is consistent with the confirmed intentional-sample design, not a complete city-zone panel. There are 35 exact duplicate raw rows, 32 unknown zones, and several flagged extreme or missing values.

Interpretation

  • Completeness is high for most measures, with missingness concentrated in speed, temperature, traffic, and zone.
  • City counts are relatively balanced. Zone counts are uneven, with Central and North dominating.
  • Temperature, parking occupancy, outage minutes, and speed show visible flagged extremes that should remain separated from ordinary descriptive summaries.
  • The dashboard describes the sample only. It does not support population-wide city rankings or causal conclusions.
